==== 2026-06-12 (session "curvature") — curved spacetime geometries in ABCMB ====

Branch: worktree-curvature (worktree at .claude/worktrees/curvature, based on

targeting <= 2e-3 agreement with CLASS (ell=2 exempt).

Design (full notes: notes_curvature/design.md + three research reports):
- params['omega_k'] (= Omega_k h^2, CLASS sign: >0 open) + derived params['K'];
  closure subtracts omega_k. Curvature enters the background as a
  Curvature(BackgroundFluid) pseudo-fluid (rho ~ a^-2, w = -1/3) — H, aH_prime
  automatically correct, zero background-machinery changes.
- Perturbations: CLASS-verbatim (arXiv:1305.3261) s_l = sqrt(1-K(l^2-1)/k^2)
  factors in all three hierarchies, g = tau*cot_K(tau) truncations, (k^2-3K)
  Einstein constraints + K h'/2 in eta' (both copies), s2^2 = 1-3K/k^2 in all
  adiabatic ICs. Always-on, value-level: every factor is exactly 1.0/+0.0 at
  K=0 in the original evaluation order (flat results preserved).
- Spectrum: static specs['curvature'] switch. Curved path generates exact
  hyperspherical Bessel Phi_l^nu(chi) by dimensionful forward recurrence
  (closed-form seeds; smooth through K=0) in one chunked checkpointed lax.scan
  over ALL integer ells, contracting LOS sources per l (no sparse-ell spline).
  Evanescent region clamped+masked at the flat tables' |Phi|~1e-10 edges in
  the variable q*S_K(chi). Radial functions T0/T1/T2/E per CLASS
  transfer_radial_function. Closed: l<=nu-1 termination via sqrt-arg masks,
  continuum-q approximation (no integer-nu snapping yet); open: q^2<=0 modes
  zero-weighted. specs['omega_k_ref'] (static) raises the k-grid floor
  (closed REQUIRES it: k_min = sqrt(8K), nu=3).
- Lensing: curved-sky Limber for Clpp (q=(l+1/2)/S_K, sin_K window,
  (1-K l^2/q^2)^(-1/2) WKB amplitude), curved Poisson k^3/(k^2-3K)^2,
  Omega_k(1+z)^2 in Om(z). Wigner-d machinery untouched.
- AD-safety: sin_K/cot_K via entire functions of K chi^2 (series near 0 —
  correct d/dK at the flat point); all sqrt/division dead branches clipped
  per landmine patterns.
